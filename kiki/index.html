Make, place, and click your own keycaps.

Kiki is a custom keycap haptic clicker for iPhone and iPad. Create photo keycaps, arrange them in frames, and enjoy responsive sound and haptics.

Jellies and rewarded ads

Open My → My Jellies to view your balance. A completed rewarded ad grants one jelly. If an ad is temporarily unavailable, wait briefly and try again.

Photo keycaps

Photo keycaps are processed on your device. Kiki does not collect face geometry or biometric face data.
