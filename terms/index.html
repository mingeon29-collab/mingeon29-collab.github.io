Terms & Community Guidelines

Effective July 22, 2026

Pipo is a scheduled short voice message that arrives for one friend at the time selected by the sender.

Prohibited content

Do not send threats, harassment, hateful or discriminatory content, sexual content, exposed personal information, impersonation, illegal material, or anything that infringes another person’s rights.

Safety controls

Recipients can report a Pipo and block its sender. Blocking cancels future Pipos between both accounts. Reports may result in content restrictions, account limits or account removal.

Device conditions

Playback depends on the recipient’s iPhone permissions, sound settings, connectivity during delivery, and supported iOS version. Senders must confirm the friend and time before sending.

Subscriptions

Unlimited sending is offered as an Apple auto-renewable subscription. The price, any trial, and renewal terms shown in the App Store purchase sheet control. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the App Store.

한국어 이용 기준

Pipo는 정한 시간에 친구 한 명에게 도착하는 예약 음성 메시지입니다. 위협, 괴롭힘, 혐오·차별, 성적 콘텐츠, 개인정보 노출, 사칭, 불법 행위, 타인의 권리를 침해하는 콘텐츠를 전송할 수 없습니다. 받은 사람은 신고와 차단을 할 수 있으며, 차단하면 두 사용자 사이의 향후 예약도 취소됩니다.
