Privacy Policy

Effective: July 27, 2026

StayClose processes distance and direction between two iPhones on-device. It does not collect your precise GPS location.

1. Information we process

  • On-device data: Your display name, alert distance, connection state, and measurements are stored on your device to provide the app.
  • Connection history: When anonymous cloud sync is used, partner name, start/end time, alert distance, sample count, average/maximum distance, alert count, and optional notes may be sent to Supabase.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process approximate location based on IP address, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and performance data for ads, fraud prevention, and measurement.

2. Why we use it

To discover and connect nearby devices, show distance and direction, deliver distance alerts, sync history, provide interstitial ads, and improve reliability.

3. Device-to-device connection

We use Apple's Multipeer Connectivity and Nearby Interaction. A connection begins only after both people consent, and the peer session is encrypted.

4. Ads and choices

The first connection each day is free. Before starting a second or later connection on the same day, the app shows a short interstitial ad. If an ad cannot be loaded, that finding session opens without making the user wait. Where required, Google's privacy choices are available again from the Me tab. The app does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

5. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until the app is removed or its data is reset. To request access, correction, or deletion of synced records, contact us below.

6. Service providers

We use technologies from Apple, Google AdMob, and Supabase, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy.

7. Children

StayClose is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
