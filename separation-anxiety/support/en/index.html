StayClose Support

Try these steps if connection or direction isn't working as expected.

I can't see the other iPhone

Open StayClose on both iPhones and allow Bluetooth, Local Network, and Nearby Interaction access. Both people must start finding; one sends a request and the other accepts.

Direction doesn't appear immediately

After connecting supported iPhones, hold them upright and move a few steps. Distance may appear first depending on the environment and device orientation.

The alert keeps vibrating

Once you return within the selected distance and readings stabilize, the alert stops automatically. If it continues, end and restart the connection on both devices.

Finding didn't unlock after an ad

Check your network and try again. The ad must finish to unlock an additional finding session. If no ad can load, the app opens finding so you are not blocked.
